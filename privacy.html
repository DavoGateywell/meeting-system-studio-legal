Privacy Policy

Last updated: 15 November 2026

What we access

If you choose to sign in with Google, Meeting System Studio requests access only to the Google data needed for its features (such as your calendar events) and your basic profile.

How data is used

Google data is used solely to provide the app's features to you. Authorization tokens are stored locally on your device. We do not sell, share, or transfer your Google user data to third parties, and we do not use it for advertising.

Storage and retention

Data stays on your device. You can remove the app's access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions and delete local data by signing out or uninstalling the app.

Google API Services

The use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
